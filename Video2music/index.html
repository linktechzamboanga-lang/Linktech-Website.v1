📖 How To Use This Converter

1. Select your video

Upload a video file from your device. Wait until its preview and information appear.

2. Select audio format

MP3 is recommended for music and sharing. WAV is suitable for uncompressed audio editing. M4A offers AAC audio compression.

3. Customize your music

Adjust volume, choose stereo or mono, and set the start and end points if you only need a specific section.

4. Apply effects

Enable fade-in or fade-out to create smoother audio beginnings and endings.

5. Convert and download

Press Convert Video to Music. When completed, preview your audio and save it to your device.

ℹ️ Format Information

MP3

Widely supported on phones, computers, music players and social platforms.

WAV

Uncompressed PCM audio. Usually larger files, useful for audio editing.

M4A

AAC compressed audio, commonly used on modern devices and media players.

🔐 Your Privacy Matters

Your selected video is processed locally in your browser using FFmpeg.wasm. This app does not send your video to a LinkTech server. The FFmpeg engine itself is downloaded from its CDN. Your browser may retain temporary processing data until it is released.
